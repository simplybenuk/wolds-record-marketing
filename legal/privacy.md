# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

Benjamin Whitfield-Heap, trading as Wolds Record ("Wolds Record", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy applies to both:

- the Wolds Record marketing website (woldsrecord.com)
- the Wolds Record application (app.woldsrecord.com)

## Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate and support our website and application, including:

- Account details (such as name and email address)
- Usage and diagnostic data
- Support communications
- Payment records, including payment and refund status, amounts and currency, and Stripe customer, subscription, connected account, Checkout session and payment intent references. We do not receive or store card details.

## How we use information

We use data to:

- Provide and maintain Wolds Record
- Improve service quality, reliability, and product experience
- Respond to support requests
- Send system-generated service communications
- Manage Wolds Record plan billing and, where enabled by a practitioner, client payments for bookings
- Meet legal and compliance obligations

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers that help us run the platform under appropriate safeguards.

### Service providers

We use trusted third-party providers, including:

- Supabase – database and authentication infrastructure
- Vercel – hosting infrastructure
- Stripe – Wolds Record plan billing and, where enabled, client payments for bookings through Stripe Connect and Stripe Checkout, as described below
- Resend – system-generated transactional emails
- Google Analytics 4 (GA4) – marketing website analytics (with consent)
- PostHog – product analytics in the application

### Stripe payments

For Wolds Record plan billing, we send Stripe the organisation's identifier, selected plan and price reference, billing interval, any offer, checkout source and, where one exists, Stripe customer reference. Stripe collects the payer's contact, billing and card details directly through its hosted checkout.

For client payments for bookings, the practitioner is the merchant and the controller of their clients' personal data. Wolds Record processes booking payment data on the practitioner's behalf. When a practitioner sets up payments, Wolds Record creates an account for their practice through Stripe Connect, sending the practice name, the owner's or administrator's contact email address and country (United Kingdom). Stripe collects any further business, identity and bank details directly through its hosted onboarding.

When a dog owner pays, Wolds Record sends Stripe the client's email address, service name, appointment date and time, amount and currency, and an internal payment identifier. Payment takes place through Stripe Checkout on the practitioner's own Stripe account. Card details go directly to Stripe; Wolds Record does not receive or store them. Wolds Record stores payment and refund status, amounts and Stripe references to show payment progress and reconcile payments and refunds.

Stripe acts as a processor for some payment activities and as an independent controller for purposes such as fraud prevention and meeting its legal obligations, as explained in its [data processing agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/dpa) and [privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

Stripe may process personal data outside the UK, including in the United States. Its [data transfers addendum](https://stripe.com/legal/dta) provides safeguards, including the Data Privacy Framework where applicable, or standard contractual clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Stripe retains personal data while providing its services and may keep it afterwards for legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, tax, accounting and reporting purposes. Retention depends on the data and applicable obligations, as described in [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

### Booking emails

When booking emails are enabled, Wolds Record uses Resend as a subprocessor to send them on behalf of practitioners. Resend receives the recipient's email address and the appointment details included in the email. Resend stores email and log data in the United States for 30 days. International transfers are covered by Resend's [data processing addendum](https://resend.com/legal/dpa), which incorporates standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum where applicable.

### Wording for practitioners' privacy notices

Practitioners are responsible for their own privacy notices as controllers of their clients' personal data. Update your notice before taking client payments through Stripe. You can adapt the following wording to reflect the booking emails and payment services you use:

We use Wolds Record to manage appointments and send booking emails. Wolds Record uses Resend to deliver these emails. Resend receives your email address and the appointment details included in the email, and stores email and log data in the United States for 30 days. Transfers are covered by contractual safeguards, including standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum where applicable.

If you pay for an appointment online, we are the merchant and use Stripe as our payment provider through our own Stripe account. Wolds Record creates this account through Stripe Connect and processes booking payment data on our behalf. Wolds Record sends Stripe your email address, the service name, appointment date and time, amount and currency, and an internal payment identifier. You enter your card details directly in Stripe Checkout; Wolds Record does not receive or store them. Wolds Record stores payment and refund status, amounts and Stripe references to help us manage payments and refunds.

Stripe also acts as an independent controller for some purposes, including fraud prevention and legal compliance. Stripe may process your data outside the UK, including in the United States, under safeguards described in its [data transfers addendum](https://stripe.com/legal/dta), including the Data Privacy Framework where applicable, or standard contractual clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Stripe retains data to provide its services and may keep it afterwards for legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, tax, accounting and reporting purposes. See [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for details.
