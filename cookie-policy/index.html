Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-23

This Cookie Policy explains how Benjamin Whitfield-Heap, trading as Wolds Record ("Wolds Record", "we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on:

  • woldsrecord.com (marketing site)
  • app.woldsrecord.com (application)

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work correctly and provide information about how sites are used.

Cookies we use

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Necessary cookies – required for core site and application functionality (for example, remembering your cookie consent preferences).
  • Analytics cookies – optional cookies used to understand aggregate usage and improve performance.

Analytics cookies and tools

If you consent to Analytics cookies, we use:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) on the marketing site (woldsrecord.com)
  • PostHog in the application (app.woldsrecord.com)

These tools help us understand traffic patterns, product usage, and improve content and user experience.

Consent controls

We use a consent banner to manage cookie choices. Analytics cookies are disabled by default and are only enabled when you explicitly accept them.

You can update your preferences at any time using the cookie settings controls shown on the site.

How long cookies are stored

Cookie duration varies by purpose. Some cookies are session-based and expire when you close your browser, while others persist for a defined period unless deleted earlier.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking some cookies may impact website or application functionality.

Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
