How to use the code

  1. Sign in or create a free account at app.woldsrecord.com.
  2. Open your organisation settings and go to Billing.
  3. Choose Starter or Pro and select annual billing.
  4. At checkout, select Add promotion code, enter IAAT and check the discount appears before you pay.

Offer terms

  • Who can use it: members of the International Association of Animal Therapists (IAAT).
  • What you get: 50% off the first year of an annual Starter or Pro subscription.
  • Annual plans only: the code does not apply to monthly billing.
  • First year only: each organisation gets the discount once, on its first annual subscription. It is not an ongoing discount.
  • Renewal: your subscription renews automatically each year at the full annual price that applies at the time. You can cancel at any time before it renews.
  • New subscriptions: enter the code when you first subscribe through checkout. It cannot be added to a subscription you already have, or combined with other offers.
  • Prices and tax: prices are in GBP. Checkout shows the full amount you will pay, including any tax, before you confirm.
  • Changes: the offer is available to IAAT members on an ongoing basis. We may change or withdraw it for new subscriptions. Any discount you have already received is not affected.
