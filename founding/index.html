Questions

Isn't Wolds Record already free?

The basic plan is, and anyone can sign up. Founding Practitioners get the upgraded plan free for 12 months, plus a setup call and a direct say in what we build. No card needed.

Is my clients' data safe?

Wolds Record is a secure, web-based app, and you stay in control of your records. You can read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy, or email us with any specific questions.

What if something's missing that I need?

Tell us. That's the point of the founding group. Some features, like scheduling, are still being built, and founding feedback decides what comes next.

Can I bring my existing records?

Yes. We'll talk through what you have on your setup call and help you get your key dogs and clients in.

What happens after 12 months?

Your account and records stay yours. You can carry on with the upgraded plan at the normal price or drop back to the free basic plan. We'll remind you well before anything changes, and there's no obligation.
